## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a polished first-concept brand design board for "Kestri", a personal AI assistant. Landscape composition, warm ivory paper background, exceptionally clean flat vector-like illustration, professionally art-directed with generous whitespace. This is one cohesive identity, not multiple unrelated options.
Main mascot large on the left: an original charming small kestrel bird, perched calmly on its own small feet, three-quarter front view, subtly tilted head, alert but patient expression. Rounded compact body without being chubby, short folded wings, recognizably small hooked beak, two understated dark vertical cheek markings, tapered moderately long tail with dark tip. Warm terracotta orange head and wings, creamy breast, deep charcoal ink eyes and restrained outlines, tiny muted blue-gray wing accents. Small intelligent dark eyes with minimal highlights, not enormous anime eyes. No accessories. The character feels like a capable quiet companion, not a fierce raptor or childish toy. Beautiful simple silhouette, minimal feather details, impeccable geometry and tasteful asymmetric pose.
On the upper right show a simplified head icon derived exactly from this mascot in a rounded square warm ivory tile, next to the lowercase wordmark "kestri" in a refined friendly rounded humanist sans serif, charcoal ink. Below this show the same icon as a small monochrome charcoal silhouette on ivory and an ivory mark on charcoal, to demonstrate small-size recognizability.
Along the bottom right show three small consistent full-body poses of the exact same bird: curious head tilt, gentle hovering with wings open, resting with wings folded. Keep facial design, colors, proportions identical across every depiction.
Four small color swatches subtly at bottom: terracotta, cream, charcoal, muted blue-gray.
Only text on the board: "kestri". No slogans, labels, fake UI, watermarks, gradients, photorealism, 3D rendering, robots, circuits, helmets, eyeglasses, books or bags. Keep all board elements aligned, uncluttered, comfortably spaced. Make the mascot distinctive and charming with restraint.

## Simplification edit prompt

Edit this Kestri brand board to simplify the illustration style substantially while preserving the warm terracotta / cream / charcoal / blue-gray palette, kestrel identity, layout, exact lowercase "kestri" wordmark, main bird pose and three small pose concepts. Transform every bird consistently into a clean minimal brand mascot built from bold smooth flat shapes. Remove ALL individual feather textures, scalloped feather edges, breast speckles, realistic shading, gradients and multi-layer wing feathers. A bird should have about 7 to 10 major flat shapes. Smooth head contour; smaller simple black oval eyes with at most one tiny white highlight, no iris rings. Keep the small hooked beak, distinctive dark vertical kestrel cheek marks, folded wings and long tail. Compact rounded body and short simplified feet. Curious, quietly capable, friendly but not infantile. Main bird should feel like a professionally designed mascot, not a realistic bird illustration. The small icon should be a truly simplified head mark readable at 24 pixels; monochrome versions must use the same geometry. Hovering pose wings should be broad smooth shapes with at most three finger-like tips each, no detailed feathers. Crisp flat vector aesthetic with a very subtle handmade character, generous whitespace. All depictions must share one recognizable character. No additional text, accessories, shadows or decorations.
